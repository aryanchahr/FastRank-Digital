What's included

What you get
with AI Automation.

Instant lead follow-up

Automatic replies by email, SMS or WhatsApp the moment an enquiry comes in.

CRM workflows

Leads created, tagged and assigned automatically, with reminders so nothing is missed.

Chat agents

A website assistant that answers common questions and captures contact details.

Lead routing

Enquiries sent to the right person based on service, location or value.

Reporting automation

Regular reports delivered to your inbox without manual spreadsheets.

Custom integrations

Connect the tools you already use so data flows without copy and paste.

Best for

  • Teams that lose leads because replies are slow
  • Businesses drowning in manual admin and data entry
  • Companies already using a CRM, forms or booking tools

What we need from you

  • A walkthrough of how enquiries and admin currently work
  • Access to the tools you want connected
  • A decision on what the automation should never do without a person

How we approach automation

  • Map how enquiries and admin currently flow through your business
  • Pick the tasks that waste the most time
  • Build and test the automation on a small scale
  • Launch with a human fallback for edge cases
  • Monitor and refine as your needs change
