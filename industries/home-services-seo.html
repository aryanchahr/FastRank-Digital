Contractor FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Services SEO.

Why am I not ranking in neighboring towns 10 miles away?

Google heavily weighs physical proximity to your verified business address. To rank in adjacent towns, you need distinct geo-targeted service pages, localized testimonials, neighborhood project photos, and localized inbound links. We implement this full geo-expansion framework for you.

Can you manage Google Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed) for us?

Yes. We handle license verification, insurance submission, dispute refunds for invalid leads, and optimize your bidding to keep your cost per qualified call as low as possible.

How do you track whether SEO is actually generating phone calls?

We install dynamic call tracking (CallRail) and form analytics. Every call is tracked with source keyword, recording, and duration, so you know exactly which campaigns generated every dollar of revenue.
