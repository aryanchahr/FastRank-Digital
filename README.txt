RANKS FUEL - deploy notes
=========================
1. Upload everything in this folder to your GitHub repo (replace the old files). Vercel redeploys automatically.

2. ENQUIRY EMAILS (one-time activation, required)
   All forms (contact, free audit, call booking, newsletter) send to aryanchahar711@gmail.com via FormSubmit.co.
   After the site is live, submit any form once yourself. FormSubmit emails aryanchahar711@gmail.com an
   "Activate Form" link. Click it. Until then, visitors will see the "could not send" message.
   (Check Spam/Promotions if it does not arrive.)

3. Replace placeholders before launch: case studies, testimonials, statistics, timeline, FAQ answers (assets/app.js data
   is generated by build, edit the page text directly in the HTML files or ask for changes).

4. After launch: add the site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit https://ranksfuel.com/sitemap.xml

5. SERVICES: 29 services in 6 categories, each service on its own page in /services/<name>.
   Digital Marketing: seo, local-seo, google-ads, social-media, pr-content-marketing, whatsapp-marketing, lead-generation, performance-marketing
   Development: web-development, ecommerce-website, wordpress-website, shopify-website, mobile-app-development, software-development
   AI & Automation: ai-automation, ai-chatbots, marketing-automation, ecommerce-automation
   Branding & Design: brand-identity, logo-design, ui-ux-design, printing
   Business Solutions: saas-product-development, custom-software-development, custom-crm-development, custom-erp-development
   Content Writing: article-writing, seo-writing, resume-writing
   /services is the overview. Five categories have their own page (/services/digital-marketing, /services/development,
   /services/branding, /services/business-solutions, /services/content-writing); AI & Automation uses /services/ai-automation.
   Only keep a service if you really offer it. If you stop offering one, remove its page, its menu and footer entries and
   its sitemap line together, and add a redirect for the old address in vercel.json so nobody lands on an error page.

6. LOCATIONS: /locations is the hub and /locations/<country> has a page for each of the 12 countries
   (UK, USA, India, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore, Ireland, New Zealand, South Africa, Germany, Netherlands).
   Each page has its own market notes, rules, cities and FAQs so Google sees genuinely different content.
   Only add a country when you can really serve it, and write it as a new unique page, not a copy.

7. HEADER MENU: Services and Locations have a mega menu (hover or keyboard on desktop, accordion on mobile).
   Services are grouped by category. The menu, footer and mobile menu are repeated in every page's HTML, so a new page
   must be added to each of them (look for the <nav class="links"> block, the mobile menu and the footer).

8. FONTS: Inter is now self-hosted in /assets/fonts (no Google Fonts requests), which is faster and avoids sending
   visitor IP addresses to Google, a known GDPR concern in Germany.

9. PRIVACY: /privacy is a template written from what the site does today. Have it reviewed by a qualified adviser and
   update it if you add analytics, ad pixels or new tools.

10. STILL PLACEHOLDERS: case studies, testimonials, statistics (8+ years, 140+ businesses, 94% retention, 12 countries),
    blog posts and the process timeline. Replace them with real figures before relying on them for SEO or ads.

11. SEARCH CONSOLE, "Page indexing" report. What the reasons mean:
    - Not found (404): Google asked for an address that does not exist. Click the row to see the exact URLs.
      If one is an old page that should now forward somewhere, add it under "redirects" in vercel.json.
      If not, leave it: 404 is the correct answer and Google drops it by itself. Then press "Validate fix".
    - Page with redirect: normal. http://, www., .html and trailing-slash versions of an address forward to the main
      address (https://ranksfuel.com/...), so Google lists them here. No action needed.
    - Discovered - currently not indexed: Google knows the page and has queued it. A new site waits a few weeks.
      Speed it up with URL inspection > Request indexing on the most important pages (about 10 a day) and by earning
      real links (Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories, partners).
    vercel.json also sends www.ranksfuel.com to ranksfuel.com, in case you attach the www address in Vercel.

12. PORTFOLIO: /portfolio shows 18 projects with category filters, and each project has its own page in /portfolio/<name>.
    The home page shows six of them and the matching service pages show up to three "Concept work" cards.
    IMPORTANT: all 18 are CONCEPT projects: invented brands designed to show how we work. Every card and page says
    "Concept", and none carries a client name, a result or a testimonial. Do not describe them as client work.
    To show a real project instead: save its screenshots in /assets/img/portfolio/ (cover 1040x650 WebP, full page 1280 px wide WebP,
    and a 1200x630 JPEG ending in -og.jpg that WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Facebook show when the page is shared),
    update its card (portfolio.html, index.html and any service page that shows it) and its own page, and remove the "Concept"
    badges and notes only for work that is real and that the client agrees to show.
    Concepts: Kestrel Legal, Pursa, Lumio Coffee Roasters, Taskwick, Rallyhouse Gym, Askwren, Elmstead Home, The Margin Journal, Tidewater Surf Co., Stridewell, Binwright, Rye & Rowan Bakery, Wildmoor Tours, Nextrung, Verde Botanics, Pocketplate, Pipewell, Leadferry.
